QHSE management systems and ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 audit readiness

You call me when

An audit is coming, and you are not sure what it will find. The system is well documented, but site practice has drifted from it.

  • A tender or client now requires a certified management system.
  • Several sites work in different ways under one system.

The work we do together

  1. Scope the standards, sites and processes, then review documents and records.
  2. Sample practice at the workface through interviews and evidence checks.
  3. Grade the gaps against the clauses and agree what matters most.
  4. Agree a prioritised plan with owners. Optionally check progress before the certification audit.

What you receive

Gap assessment report

You will know which requirements the auditor is most likely to question, what evidence exists at the workface today, and what to fix first.

Also included

  • Prioritised action plan with owners and sequence
  • Revised procedures and forms people can use on site
  • Audit-readiness briefing for managers and process owners

I prepare you for audit and do not issue certificates. An accredited certification body decides certification.

Project and contractor assurance, from mobilisation to close-out

You call me when

A contractor is about to mobilise, and you want to know they are ready before the first shift, not after the first finding.

  • Several contractors share an area and their standards differ.
  • Findings keep repeating and actions are not closing.

The work we do together

  1. Agree the requirements and readiness criteria for the contractor and the work.
  2. Run a readiness review before work starts.
  3. Visit the site and verify the work against what was agreed.
  4. Review findings with the contractor and project team, then follow corrective actions until they close.

What you receive

Contractor readiness review

Before mobilisation you will know whether the contractor's people, equipment, method statements and supervision meet the agreed requirements, and which items must close before work starts.

Also included

  • Field verification reports with evidence
  • Corrective-action tracker with owners and dates agreed with you
  • Escalation summary for the project director

HSE leadership and workforce training

You call me when

Advisers raise concerns that are noted, and then nothing happens. Supervisors were promoted for technical skill and now have to lead people.

  • Contractor engagement is a list of rules rather than a working relationship.
  • Briefings are delivered but not remembered.

The work we do together

  1. A short scoping conversation about the situations your people actually face.
  2. A workshop built around realistic site conversations, with role rehearsal and feedback.
  3. Each participant commits to one practical action for the next shift.
  4. An optional follow-up session to review what happened.

What you receive

A workshop built for your project

Each participant leaves having rehearsed one real conversation, such as a difficult conversation with a contractor, and committed to one action for the next shift.

Also included

  • Practice sheets and handouts for participants
  • A short summary of themes and recommended follow-up
  • Delivery in English, French or Arabic; length agreed with you

Practical AI for construction safety and quality teams

You call me when

Your team already uses AI tools informally, or wants to, and nobody is sure who checks the output.

  • You want site reports, contractor reviews and follow-up drafted faster.
  • You worry about invented facts or confidential data in AI tools.

The work we do together

  1. Pick two or three routine tasks where AI could help.
  2. Work through them with your own non-confidential examples.
  3. Build prompts and workflows with named review checkpoints.
  4. Agree who verifies each output against its source before it is used.

What you receive

Workflow guide with a review checklist

Your team will know which tasks AI may draft, who verifies each output against the source, and what must never be entered into an AI tool.

Also included

  • Hands-on workshop for your team
  • Tested prompt templates
  • Simple team rules on what may go into AI tools

AI output is a draft. A person stays accountable for every document that leaves your team. See also the book AI for Construction Safety Managers.
