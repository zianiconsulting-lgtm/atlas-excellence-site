The Effective HSE Professional

Field guides for HSE professionals who need to influence decisions, speak up and follow through. Each book follows realistic site conversations and gives you tools to practise on your next shift.

Book 1, The Effective HSE Professional

Influence Without Authority for HSE Professionals

Available in paperback and Kindle

Practical Conversations, Stakeholder Tools and Follow-Through for Busy Projects

A safety adviser raises a concern and the people who can act have other priorities. Follow Samira, an HSE adviser on a fictional construction project, as a concern that was noted becomes a decision with owners, conditions and follow-up. Each chapter pairs a conversation that falls short with a better one, plus a practical tool, a completed example, a blank sheet and a role-play. Urgent danger is acted on at once; the book is for planned improvements that need other people's decisions.

Book 2, The Effective HSE Professional

Difficult Conversations in HSE

Available in paperback and Kindle

What to Say When Facts Are Disputed, Trust Is Strained and the Record Matters

Some of the hardest moments in health and safety are not technical: a supervisor says you got it wrong in front of the team, or a manager asks you to soften a finding. Follow Samira, an HSE adviser on a fictional construction project, through ten difficult conversations. Each pairs a conversation that falls short with a better one, plus a practical tool, a blank sheet and a rehearsal. Urgent protection always comes first.

Book 3, The Effective HSE Professional

From Safety Policing to Practical Coaching

Available in paperback and Kindle

Helping People See, Judge and Fix Risks for Themselves

Many walk-rounds follow the same pattern: the adviser finds the fault, points it out, and next week it is back. This book shows how to turn ordinary checks and start-of-shift conversations into short, practical coaching, so the people doing the work see, judge and fix more of the risk themselves. Ten situations, each with a tool, a completed example, a blank sheet and a rehearsal.
